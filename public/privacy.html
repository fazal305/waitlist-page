Privacy note

Last updated 7 October 2026

Shelfnote is a fictional product. This page is a portfolio project by Fazal Abbas that demonstrates a working waitlist form. Here is exactly what happens to the data you submit.

What we store

  • The email address you enter, and the time you joined.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address, kept for at most 24 hours, used only to limit repeated submissions. Your raw IP address is not stored.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t send you email. There is no launch, newsletter or mailing list.
  • We don’t sell, share or rent your address.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

Where it lives

Submissions are stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database that is not publicly readable. The site is hosted on Vercel. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when serving them.

Removal

To have your email deleted, write to fazalabbas2002@gmail.com from that address and it will be removed.
